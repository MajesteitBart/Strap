# Decisions: Strap Visual Redesign

## Active Decisions
- 2026-09-13: The user approved the existing `/home` and `/docs` worktable direction for every remaining surface in this contract: warm paper, flat white surfaces, crisp one-pixel dark borders, stable resource colours, display typography with compact monospace labels, responsive layouts, accessible focus, and reduced-motion behaviour. Public inner pages, authentication, onboarding, OAuth consent, device authorization, invitations, transactional email, and the signed-in Personal and Company product adopt this system. No further direction approval is required for T-001 through T-005.
- 2026-09-13: Shared tokens and primitives land first (T-004), then surfaces roll out by independently verifiable group. Product semantics, permissions, persistence, agent contracts, migrations, and compatibility identifiers stay unchanged.
- 2026-09-13: The legacy sky-image marketing chrome and its backdrop assets are retired once every consumer moves to the worktable shell; the theme regression test is updated to assert their absence rather than weakened.
