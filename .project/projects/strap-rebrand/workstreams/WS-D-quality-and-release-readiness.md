# Workstream: WS-D Quality and Release Readiness

## Objective

Prove the rebrand is complete, compatible, accessible, buildable, and ready for operator-controlled release.

## Owned Files/Areas

- Brand coverage audit and focused/full automated verification.
- Fable plan/diff reviews and multi-model agent-contract checks.
- Computer-use desktop/mobile browser smoke evidence.
- Delano quality bundle, outcome review, and closeout.

## Dependencies

- Completed implementation workstreams.
- A runnable local or preview build and available authenticated test states.

## Risks

- Browser tooling or authenticated fixtures are unavailable.
- Mechanical checks either miss visible copy or flag stable internal identifiers.
- External domain/package state is mistaken for local completion.

## Handoff Criteria

- Critical checks and Fable blockers are clear and resolved.
- GUI evidence covers the required public and available signed-in routes.
- Remaining operator-gated release actions are explicit and no external mutation is implied.
